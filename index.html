How to View and Search This Page

Opening This File

Searching for Products (Search Bar)

At the top of the page you'll see a search box that says "Search products by name, ID, description, or category..."

  1. Click inside the search box.
  2. Type what you're looking for (for example, "sensor" or "ESP32").
  3. The product list updates instantly as you type — only matching items will remain visible.
  4. To clear your search, delete the text in the box or click the × at the right end of the search field.

Filtering by Supplier

Below the search box there are buttons labelled All, Adafruit, Pimoroni, etc.

  • Click a supplier button to show only products from that supplier.
  • Click All to show everything again.
  • Filters work together with the search — you can search within a specific supplier.

Using Your Browser's Built-in Search

Every web browser has a built-in "find on page" feature:

  • Windows / Linux: Press Ctrl + F
  • Mac: Press ⌘ Cmd + F

A small search bar will appear (usually at the top or bottom of the window). Type a word and the browser will highlight every match on the page and let you jump between them with arrow buttons.

Viewing Product Details

  • Click a product image or title to open its product page on the supplier's website (opens in a new tab).
  • Click "Technical Specs" on a card to expand and read specifications.
  • Click "Product Page" or "Learn Guide" links at the bottom of a card for more information.

Scrolling Through Products

  • Mouse: Use the scroll wheel, or click and drag the scrollbar on the right side of the window.
  • Trackpad: Swipe up or down with two fingers.
  • Keyboard: Press Space to scroll down, or Shift + Space to scroll up. Home jumps to the top, End jumps to the bottom.
